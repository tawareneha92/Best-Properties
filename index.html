How It Works

Four Steps From First Call to Registered Keys

A property purchase in Pune has roughly forty moving parts. Here is how we keep them in order for you.

  1. Free consultation

    Thirty minutes on your budget, loan eligibility, commute and timeline. You leave with a realistic price band for the areas you are considering, whether or not you engage us.

  2. Curated shortlist

    We match your brief against verified inventory and send five to eight options with honest notes on each, including the ones we would rule out and why.

  3. Site visits & due diligence

    We accompany every visit, then check title, approvals, RERA status, society dues and encumbrance before you release a single rupee of token money.

  4. Negotiation to possession

    Price and terms negotiated on your behalf, loan and valuation coordinated, agreement and registration scheduled, handover inspected with you present.

Common Questions

What Buyers Ask Us First

Straight answers on fees, timelines, loans and NRI purchases. Anything not covered here, ask on the call.

What does it cost to work with a property consultant in Pune?
The first consultation is free and carries no obligation. Beyond that, brokerage is charged on successful transactions only, agreed in writing before we begin, and quoted as a flat figure so you know your total cost upfront. Advisory-only engagements such as appraisals and portfolio reviews are billed as a fixed fee.
Do you help first-time home buyers with loans?
Yes. We assess your eligibility before the search begins, so your shortlist matches what a bank will actually sanction. We coordinate documentation, valuation and sanction with your preferred lender, and can introduce you to lenders if you do not have one.
Can you manage a property while I live outside Pune?
Property management is one of our core services. Tenant sourcing and screening, agreement and registration, rent collection, maintenance coordination, periodic inspections and photo reports. NRI clients receive documentation support for power of attorney and repatriation-related paperwork.
How long does a typical purchase take?
For a ready-to-move resale flat with a home loan, six to ten weeks from shortlist to registration is realistic. Under-construction purchases depend on the builder's milestones. Plots and commercial transactions vary with title complexity, which we flag at the shortlist stage rather than at agreement.
Which parts of Pune do you cover?
We work across Pune city and the surrounding growth corridors, including the western and eastern residential belts, the expressway stretch and the industrial and warehousing pockets on the city's edge. If a locality is outside our direct coverage, we will tell you rather than guess.
