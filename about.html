Our Story

Built From the Questions Buyers Were Afraid to Ask

Best Properties began because too many buyers in Pune were signing agreements they had not read, for flats they had seen once, at prices nobody had benchmarked.

We rebuilt the process around the part that actually protects you: due diligence before excitement. Title, approvals, RERA registration, society dues, encumbrance and true carpet area are checked before you are asked for token money, not after.

Today the practice covers residential and commercial property sales, rentals, land, investment advisory and property management across Pune. The scale has grown. The rule that one consultant stays with you from first call to handover has not.

What We Hold To

Four Commitments You Can Hold Us To

Not aspirations. Working rules that shape what we show you and what we refuse to show you.

Verified before shown

If we cannot see the title chain and the approvals, the property does not reach your shortlist.

Priced against reality

Every quote is benchmarked against recent registrations in the same building or locality.

Fees agreed in writing

A flat figure, confirmed before work begins. No revision at the closing table.

The honest no

When a property is wrong for you, we say so, even when saying so costs us the transaction.
