Before You Visit

What We Check on Every Property We List

This is the file we build before a property reaches this page, and the file you receive when you shortlist it.

Title chain

Ownership traced back through prior transactions, with the encumbrance certificate on record.

Approvals & RERA

Sanctioned plans, commencement and occupancy status, and the project's RERA registration.

True area

Carpet, built-up and super built-up stated separately, so the rate you compare is the real one.

Dues & deposits

Society maintenance, sinking fund and outstanding utility positions confirmed before agreement.

Listing Questions

Before You Book a Site Visit

Are these prices negotiable?
Usually, within a band. We will tell you before the visit what recent registrations in the same building or layout have closed at, so you know where the realistic floor sits rather than negotiating blind.
Can I see a property remotely?
Yes. We record a full walkthrough with commentary on light, ventilation, road noise and finish quality, and join a live video call from the site so you can direct the camera yourself. Many of our NRI and relocating clients shortlist entirely this way.
Do you charge buyers to view properties?
No. Viewings and the accompanying diligence summary are part of the engagement. Brokerage applies only on a completed transaction, at the flat figure agreed in writing beforehand.
What if nothing here matches what I want?
That is the normal case. This page is a sample of current inventory, not our full reach. Share your brief and we will search owner listings, builder inventory, auction lots and off-market options against it.
